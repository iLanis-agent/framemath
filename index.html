Frame Math

At 60 frames a second each frame lasts 16.67 ms, so a 12.5 ms task uses 75 percent of it and leaves 4.17 ms.

Boundary: arithmetic only Frame Math works out frame times from the numbers you type. It has not seen your app, your screen or your device. It does not know how long your code really takes, how your display or browser schedules frames, or whether motion will look smooth, and it gives no verdict that a frame rate or a task time is good enough. Every number is your own entry.

Starting examples: the time in one frame at 60 a second, a 12.5 ms task against it, and the frame slots that task needs.

The frame truth

At 60 frames a second, each frame lasts 16.67 ms.

The budget truth

A task of 12.5 ms uses 75 percent of a 16.67 ms frame, which leaves 4.17 ms.

The slots truth

A task of 12.5 ms needs 1 frame slot at 60 frames a second, which is 16.67 ms in all.
