Frame Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out frame times from the numbers you type. It has not seen your app, your screen or your device. It does not know how long your code really takes, how your display or browser schedules frames, or whether motion will look smooth, and it gives no verdict that a frame rate or a task time is good enough. Every number is your own entry.
